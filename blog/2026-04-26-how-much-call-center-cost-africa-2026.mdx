# How Much Does a Call Center Cost in Africa?

If you are budgeting for outsourced customer service, the short answer
is that **call center cost in Africa** can range from roughly **\$9 to
\$16 per agent hour** in several commonly benchmarked African markets,
depending on country, service complexity and what the provider includes.

A 2026 BPO cost index lists Kenya around \$9/hour, Egypt around \$12,
South Africa and Tunisia around \$13, and Morocco around \$16 for fully
loaded voice-agent delivery. The same index puts the US around
\$35/hour. [Source](https://www.rethinkcx.com/resources/bpo-cost-index)

These figures are benchmarks, not guaranteed quotes.

## What is included in a fully loaded rate?

A genuine fully loaded BPO rate can include:

-   Agent compensation
-   Benefits
-   Team leadership
-   Quality assurance
-   Training
-   Facilities
-   Internet
-   Power and backup systems
-   Contact-center technology
-   Workforce management
-   Reporting
-   Provider margin

Always ask for an itemized pricing schedule.

## Why does call center cost vary between African countries?

### Labor market

Agent salaries are a major component of cost, but the cheapest labor
market is not automatically the cheapest operation.

### Language

Rare languages and premium language skills can increase rates.

### Complexity

Technical support, financial services, regulated work and complex sales
cost more than basic scripted customer service.

### Operating hours

Night shifts, weekends and 24/7 coverage can materially increase the
effective rate.

### Quality requirements

Strict QA, low abandonment targets, high first-contact resolution and
advanced reporting require more management and technology.

## Example budget

Suppose you need **50 agents** at an average fully loaded rate of
**\$12/hour** and 160 billable hours per agent per month.

50 × \$12 × 160 = **\$96,000 per month**

That is an illustrative service budget, not a guaranteed provider quote.

At \$9/hour, the same staffing model would be \$72,000/month. At
\$16/hour, it would be \$128,000/month.

The difference demonstrates why country and scope selection matter.

## What about setup costs?

Some providers charge separate setup fees. Others amortize onboarding
into the hourly rate.

Potential setup expenses include:

-   Recruitment
-   Training
-   CRM configuration
-   Telephony setup
-   Data migration
-   Knowledge-base creation
-   Security assessments
-   Dedicated infrastructure
-   Transition management

Ask whether the proposal includes these items or bills them separately.

## Pricing models

### Per agent hour

You pay for staffed or productive agent hours. This is common and easy
to compare.

### Per dedicated seat

You pay a monthly fee for an assigned agent or seat.

### Per interaction

You pay per call, chat, email or resolved ticket. This can work well
when volume is predictable and the provider has efficient automation.

### Outcome-based

The provider is paid partly according to sales, resolution or another
measurable outcome. This requires carefully designed contracts.

## How to get a realistic call center quote

Give providers:

1.  Expected monthly contacts
2.  Average handle time
3.  Languages
4.  Opening hours
5.  Channels
6.  Customer geography
7.  Required SLA
8.  Expected first-contact resolution
9.  QA requirements
10. Technology integrations

Without this information, an hourly quote may not be comparable.

## Africa vs the rest of the world

A 2026 benchmark puts Kenya at about \$9/hour and the US at about
\$35/hour for fully loaded outsourced voice delivery. Western Europe and
North America commonly sit substantially above African offshore markets.
[Source](https://www.rethinkcx.com/resources/bpo-cost-index)

But a lower headline rate can become less attractive if the provider has
high attrition, weak quality or unreliable infrastructure.

## Final answer

For planning purposes, a reasonable 2026 starting assumption is
**roughly \$9--\$16 per agent hour for several established African
call-center markets**, with Kenya toward the lower end and Morocco
toward the higher end of the benchmark cited above.

Treat that as a budgeting range, not a quote. The final **call center
cost Africa** buyers pay depends on country, service complexity,
languages, hours, technology, volume and contract structure.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the average call center cost in Africa?

A 2026 benchmark puts several major African destinations between about
\$9 and \$16 per fully loaded agent hour.
[Source](https://www.rethinkcx.com/resources/bpo-cost-index)

### Which African country has the cheapest call center?

Kenya appears at the low end of the 2026 benchmark, at about \$9/hour
typical, with a \$7--\$12 vendor range in the cited index.

### Does a cheap hourly rate mean a cheaper call center?

Not necessarily. Recruitment, attrition, training, quality,
infrastructure and technology can change total cost significantly.

### How much would 100 agents cost?

At \$12/hour and 160 hours per month, 100 agents would cost
approximately **\$192,000 per month** before any additional
scope-specific charges not included in the quoted rate.
