# Call Center Outsourcing Costs: Africa vs Europe vs the US

When businesses compare international call-center outsourcing, price is
usually the first question.

In 2026, the broad pattern is straightforward: **Africa generally offers
lower outsourced call-center rates than Western Europe and the US**,
while Eastern and Southern Europe occupy a middle position.

But the cheapest hourly rate is not automatically the best business
decision. Language, time zones, quality, compliance, infrastructure and
service complexity can be more important than a few dollars per hour.

## Africa: lowest cost among these major regions

The African markets in the benchmark range from about \$9/hour in Kenya
to \$16/hour in Morocco.

### Why Africa can be cheaper

-   Lower labor costs
-   Large graduate and young-professional pools
-   Competitive facility costs in many markets
-   Government incentives in several destinations
-   Growing digital infrastructure

But buyers should not assume that all African countries cost the same.

South Africa can carry a premium because of its mature CX ecosystem.
Morocco can be more expensive because French-language and
European-facing capabilities command higher rates.

## Europe: middle ground

Europe offers a wide range.

Eastern Europe can be surprisingly competitive, with Romania and
Bulgaria appearing much closer to African pricing than Western Europe.

Western European delivery is significantly more expensive but may offer
advantages in:

-   Local language fluency
-   Regulatory familiarity
-   Geographic proximity
-   Lower coordination friction
-   Specialized B2B expertise

For an EU-regulated financial-services program, a European provider may
justify a premium even when an African provider is cheaper.

## United States: highest-cost option

US onshore outsourcing generally commands the highest rate in this
comparison.

A 2026 benchmark places US outsourced call-center delivery around
\$28--\$42/hour in a common fully loaded range, with another benchmark
using about \$35/hour as a typical midpoint.
[Source](https://www.rethinkcx.com/resources/bpo-cost-index)

The US can still make sense when customers require:

-   Native local presence
-   Highly regulated support
-   Complex B2B interactions
-   Very tight US business-hour coverage
-   Specialized industry knowledge

## What does a 100-agent operation cost?

Assume 160 hours per agent per month.

### Kenya at \$9/hour

100 × 160 × \$9 = **\$144,000/month**

### South Africa at \$13/hour

100 × 160 × \$13 = **\$208,000/month**

### Romania at \$17/hour

100 × 160 × \$17 = **\$272,000/month**

### United States at \$35/hour

100 × 160 × \$35 = **\$560,000/month**

This illustrates the enormous headline cost difference. But it is only
useful if the services being compared are genuinely equivalent.

## The hidden variable: service complexity

A basic inbound customer-service queue is not equivalent to a regulated
technical-support program.

Rates increase when you add:

-   Advanced technical skills
-   Financial or healthcare compliance
-   Rare languages
-   Complex sales
-   24/7 coverage
-   Strict SLAs
-   Dedicated management
-   Advanced analytics
-   Specialized technology

One 2026 pricing guide reports a global range from roughly \$5 to
\$55/hour and notes that specialized multilingual technical support and
regulated-industry work sits above standard customer-service pricing.
[Source](https://callitdev.com/en/call-center-outsourcing-cost)

## Pricing model matters

### Hourly

Simple and predictable, but make sure you understand whether the
provider bills staffed or productive hours.

### Dedicated monthly seat

Useful for stable teams where agents work exclusively on your program.

### Per interaction

Can work well when volume and resolution definitions are clear.

### Per minute

Common for voice programs, but can make complex calls more expensive.

### Outcome-based

Potentially attractive for sales and other measurable processes, but
contracts need careful KPI definitions.

## How to compare quotes fairly

Do not compare:

> Africa: \$10/hour\
> US: \$35/hour

and stop there.

Instead ask every provider for the same scope:

-   Same hours
-   Same channels
-   Same language
-   Same SLA
-   Same training
-   Same technology
-   Same QA target
-   Same management ratio
-   Same reporting
-   Same disaster-recovery requirements

Then calculate **total cost per resolved customer issue**, not just cost
per agent hour.

## The right strategy may be blended

A large international company does not have to choose only one
geography.

A blended model might use:

-   **Africa** for high-volume standard support
-   **Eastern Europe** for specialized European-language work
-   **US** for complex or regulated customer segments

This can reduce the average cost while preserving local expertise where
it matters.

## Final verdict

The 2026 pricing pattern is clear:

**Africa generally offers the lowest fully loaded call-center costs
among the major regions compared here. Eastern Europe sits in the
middle, while Western Europe and the US command higher rates.**

Within Africa, Kenya and Egypt sit toward the lower end of the published
benchmark, South Africa occupies a middle position, and Morocco is
higher but offers strong French/European capabilities.
[Source](https://www.rethinkcx.com/resources/bpo-cost-index)

The best **call center outsourcing cost** decision is not necessarily
the lowest hourly quote. It is the geography that produces the lowest
total cost while meeting your quality, compliance and
customer-experience requirements.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does call center outsourcing cost in Africa?

Several African markets benchmark around \$9--\$16 per fully loaded
agent hour in 2026, depending on country.
[Source](https://www.rethinkcx.com/resources/bpo-cost-index)

### Is Africa cheaper than Europe for call centers?

Generally yes, especially compared with Western Europe. Eastern Europe
can be much closer to African rates.

### Is Africa cheaper than the US?

Generally by a wide margin for standard outsourced voice support. The
cited 2026 benchmark is about \$9--\$16/hour for several African
destinations versus about \$35/hour for the US.
[Source](https://www.rethinkcx.com/resources/bpo-cost-index)

### Is the cheapest outsourcing destination always best?

No. Quality, language, compliance, infrastructure, attrition and
customer satisfaction can outweigh hourly savings.

### What should I ask a call-center provider before signing?

Ask for the full pricing model, inclusions/exclusions, productive-hour
definition, SLA assumptions, staffing model, attrition, technology
costs, implementation fees, disaster recovery and termination terms.
