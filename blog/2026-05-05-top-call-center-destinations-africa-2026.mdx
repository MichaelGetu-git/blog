# Top Call Center Destinations in Africa

Africa has become an increasingly important region for international
call-center and customer-experience operations.

The continent offers English, French and Arabic-speaking talent,
competitive labor costs and time zones that can work particularly well
for European and UK customers. The strongest destinations differ
depending on whether a buyer needs premium English CX, multilingual
support, massive hiring capacity or an emerging low-cost location.

For 2026, the most useful shortlist includes **South Africa, Egypt,
Kenya, Nigeria, Morocco, Ghana and Tunisia**, with Ethiopia and Rwanda
worth watching as emerging markets.

## 1. South Africa

South Africa is one of Africa's best choices for a company that wants an
experienced international contact-center ecosystem.

Its mature CX sector supports customer care, sales, technical support
and complex service journeys. English is a major advantage, and Cape
Town and Johannesburg provide established business-services
infrastructure.

The country's fifth-place global ranking in the 2026 Global Outsourcing
Talent Index reinforces its position as Africa's leading mature
outsourcing destination.
[Source](https://news.outsourceaccelerator.com/south-africa-nigeria-outsourcing/)

**Best for:** Premium customer experience, UK-facing support, financial
services, travel, telecoms and technical support.

## 2. Egypt

Egypt is a standout choice for multilingual call-center operations.

Arabic makes it highly relevant to Middle Eastern customers, while
English and other languages support international programs. Egypt also
offers substantial scale.

ITIDA reported that the country's offshoring sector generated \$5.2
billion in exports in 2025 and had 252 companies operating across 282
global delivery centers.
[Source](https://itida.gov.eg/English/PressReleases/Pages/egypt-pm-madbouly-mcit-minister-raafat-hindy-alexandria-visit.aspx)

**Best for:** Multilingual customer care, telecoms, banking, travel and
high-volume programs.

## 3. Kenya

Kenya has become one of the continent's leading English-language
call-center destinations.

Nairobi is the main hub, with a growing ecosystem around customer
support, data work, digital operations and technology-enabled services.

A 2026 Kenya call-center analysis reports strong English capability,
competitive delivery costs and significant potential hiring capacity.
[Source](https://outsourcing.ke/insights/call-center-kenya/)

**Best for:** English customer support, SaaS, e-commerce, technical help
desks and UK-facing operations.

## 4. Nigeria

Nigeria offers one of the deepest English-speaking labor pools in
Africa.

The market is particularly attractive for large operations that need to
recruit substantial numbers of agents or combine call-center work with
back-office and digital processes.

Nigeria ranked sixth globally in the 2026 outsourcing talent index.
[Source](https://news.outsourceaccelerator.com/south-africa-nigeria-outsourcing/)

The key issue is not talent alone. Buyers should carefully assess
connectivity, power resilience, workforce management, security and
business continuity.

**Best for:** Large English call centers, sales, customer service and
digital support.

## 5. Morocco

Morocco is a leading French-language call-center destination.

It is especially useful for European companies that need French-speaking
agents while retaining geographic proximity to Europe.

**Best for:** French customer care, travel, retail, telecoms and
financial services.

## 6. Ghana

Ghana provides another credible English-language call-center option.

Accra is the main hub. The country is smaller than Nigeria or South
Africa but can be attractive for companies that want a focused operation
with UK and European working-hour alignment.

**Best for:** Customer care, outbound calling, appointment setting and
sales support.

## 7. Tunisia

Tunisia is a good option for French and Arabic support.

Its proximity to European markets makes it useful for European
customer-service operations, particularly where language and regional
familiarity are important.

**Best for:** French/Arabic support, technical help desks and European
programs.

## 8. Ethiopia

Ethiopia is one of the most interesting emerging **call center Africa**
markets.

The country has a large workforce and an expanding digital-services
ecosystem. In 2026, Ethiopian Investment Holdings announced a \$25.56
million investment in a BPO initiative involving a planned 3,000-seat
facility.
[Source](https://cciglobal.com/elevate-africa-2026-positions-ethiopia-as-a-rising-global-outsourcing-hub)

This does not yet make Ethiopia equivalent to South Africa or Egypt in
market maturity. It does, however, indicate growing institutional
commitment and physical capacity.

**Best for:** Emerging customer-support teams, pilot programs and
cost-conscious international operations.

## 9. Rwanda

Rwanda is another emerging option.

Its smaller workforce means it is better suited to specialized programs
than massive call-center deployments. Its digital-economy ambitions make
it a market worth watching.

## What makes a great call-center destination?

### English or multilingual communication

The ability to speak a language is only the starting point. Buyers
should assess clarity, writing, cultural fluency and customer-handling
skills.

### Talent availability

Ask the provider how many agents it can hire each month, not just how
many people live in the country.

### Infrastructure

Reliable electricity and redundant connectivity are essential. A call
center cannot deliver consistent service if agents frequently lose power
or internet access.

### Time zone

South Africa, Ghana, Kenya and other African destinations can provide
useful UK/EU overlap. For US support, overnight shifts and staffing
costs must be evaluated.

### CX maturity

A mature destination usually has better workforce-management practices,
quality-assurance talent, training organizations and experienced
operations leaders.

## Call-center cost considerations

Do not compare hourly wages without considering total delivery cost.

Include:

-   Agent compensation
-   Recruitment
-   Training
-   Facilities
-   Telephony
-   CRM
-   Internet
-   Backup power
-   Management
-   QA
-   Security
-   Attrition

A 2026 African outsourcing analysis reports that fully loaded seat costs
can vary considerably between Egypt, Kenya and South Africa, with Egypt
at the lower end and South Africa carrying a premium associated with
greater CX maturity.
[Source](https://outsourcing.ke/insights/top-african-outsourcing-destinations/)

## Final verdict

The best **call center Africa** destinations are not identical for every
business.

**South Africa** is the strongest mature English option. **Egypt** is
ideal for multilingual scale. **Kenya** balances English, digital skills
and cost. **Nigeria** offers exceptional workforce scale. **Morocco and
Tunisia** are particularly useful for French and Arabic programs, while
**Ghana and Ethiopia** provide emerging alternatives.

The best destination is the one that matches your customer geography,
language, service complexity and required scale.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the top call center destinations in Africa?

South Africa, Egypt, Kenya and Nigeria are among the strongest choices,
followed by Morocco, Ghana and Tunisia. Ethiopia and Rwanda are
emerging.

### Which African country is best for English call centers?

South Africa is the leading mature option. Kenya, Nigeria and Ghana are
also strong.

### Which African country is best for French call centers?

Morocco and Tunisia are particularly strong, while Egypt offers broader
multilingual capability.

### Is Africa good for call center outsourcing?

Yes. The continent offers a combination of talent, language diversity
and competitive operating costs. The 2026 Everest Group/Teleperformance
analysis describes Africa as increasingly strategic for global service
delivery.
[Source](https://www.tp.com/en-us/insights-list/white-papers/a-comparative-analysis-of-leading-african-countries-for-global-services-delivery/)
